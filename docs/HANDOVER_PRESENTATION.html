Akram Sweets · Shareholders

A · Business idea

One place instead of spreadsheets

Akram Sweets manages the business. Shareholders invest capital. Each month, approved profit is shared under Mudarabah — fairly, on paper, and visible to each investor.

For accounts

Paste Net Profit from Odoo once. The system splits it and keeps the working.

For management

Review, approve, certify. Certificates and notices go to investors.

For investors

A portal: my month, my certificate, my ownership, request to take capital back.

A · Golden rule

Capital is not this month’s profit

Capital register — the investment

Shares, money invested, Murabaha assets, value of one share.

Shown on the dashboard and statements for information.

Changing share value does not change this month’s payout.

Monthly profit — what we calculate

Only Approved Monthly Net Profit from Odoo.

Split under Mudarabah, then by ownership %, then by any special agreement.

That is the certificate number — what you pay.

B · How profit is calculated

Four steps — every month

Investor payout = their share of the shareholders’ pool after Mudarabah — not of the full company Net Profit.
01Take Net ProfitCopy approved monthly Net Profit from Odoo. Loss = a negative number.
→
02Split the monthMudarabah (often 50/50): shareholders’ pool vs Akram Sweets as managing partner.
→
03Share the poolBase = pool × ownership %. Active ownership must add to 100%.
→
04Deals, then lockSpecial arrangements, rare one-off adjustments, then management certifies. That is final.

The company partner share is never cut by investor side-deals. Those only move money inside the pool.

B · Special arrangements

A side deal — without changing ownership

Ownership stays 30 / 40 / 30. After the pool is split, a standing rule can move part of some people’s base share to someone else (often the founding owner).

Typical deal

“The owner receives 20% of every other shareholder’s monthly share.”

Or only named people. Or profit months only, not losses.

Same 50,000 pool, 20% of others → Owner

A pays 4,000 · B pays 3,000 · Owner receives +7,000.

Final: Owner 22,000 · A 16,000 · B 12,000. Pool still 50,000. Company still 50,000.

E · How to · Finance

Enter this month’s profit

  1. Sign in as Finance (email, password, then the code if asked).
  2. If ownership is not 100%, stop — ask Admin to fix Shareholders first.
  3. Open Monthly Periods → Enter Monthly Result.
  4. Choose year and month (cannot duplicate a month).
  5. Type Approved Monthly Net Profit from Odoo. Minus for a loss. Optional journal ref.
  6. Preview the pool and each person. Then Save & Calculate.
  7. Open the month. Check base, arrangement, final. Add a manual adjustment only for a true one-off, with a reason.
  8. Submit for Review. You cannot change figures until management sends it back.
Akram Sweets · Shareholders

E · How to · Management

Approve the month and notify investors

  1. Sign in as Super Admin or System Administrator.
  2. Open Approvals (or the bell). Open the submitted month.
  3. Check: Net Profit matches Odoo, split is the agreed %, finals add to the pool.
  4. Wrong → Return to draft with a reason. Finance fixes and re-submits.
  5. Right → Approve & Certify. Certificates issue. Reports go if email/WhatsApp is on (or Send Reports Now).
  6. When cash has actually been paid: Mark payment completed (record only — no bank).
  7. To correct a locked month before payment: Reopen with a reason; it becomes draft and must be certified again.

E · How to · Special arrangement

Put the side deal on the system

  1. Owner/Admin: open Special Arrangements.
  2. Name it, e.g. “Owner 20% from others”.
  3. Recipient = an active shareholder. % = of each source’s base monthly share.
  4. All others, or tick named people. Profit and/or loss. Start date; end optional.
  5. Save. Next calculate in that date range applies it. Check on the period before you approve.

F · Honest scope

In the system vs still done outside

You use the system to

  • Share each month’s Odoo Net Profit under Mudarabah
  • Keep the register and ownership at 100%
  • Honour side bonuses without rewriting ownership
  • Certify, certificate, and notify investors
  • Record capital-return requests
  • Give each investor a private portal

You still do outside

  • Close the books in Odoo — no live link; someone types Net Profit
  • Actually pay people — “payment completed” is a tick
  • Update ownership after capital really leaves

Akram Sweets · Shareholders

A · Business idea

One place instead of spreadsheets

Akram Sweets manages the business. Shareholders invest capital. Each month, approved profit is shared under Mudarabah — fairly, on paper, and visible to each investor.

For accounts

Paste Net Profit from Odoo once. The system splits it and keeps the working.

For management

Review, approve, certify. Certificates and notices go to investors.

For investors

A portal: my month, my certificate, my ownership, request to take capital back.

A · Golden rule

Capital is not this month’s profit

Capital register — the investment

Shares, money invested, Murabaha assets, value of one share.

Shown on the dashboard and statements for information.

Changing share value does not change this month’s payout.

Monthly profit — what we calculate

Only Approved Monthly Net Profit from Odoo.

Split under Mudarabah, then by ownership %, then by any special agreement.

That is the certificate number — what you pay.

Akram Sweets · Shareholders

B · How profit is calculated

Four steps — every month

Investor payout = their share of the shareholders’ pool after Mudarabah — not of the full company Net Profit.
01Take Net ProfitCopy approved monthly Net Profit from Odoo. Loss = a negative number.
→
02Split the monthMudarabah (often 50/50): shareholders’ pool vs Akram Sweets as managing partner.
→
03Share the poolBase = pool × ownership %. Active ownership must add to 100%.
→
04Deals, then lockSpecial arrangements, rare one-off adjustments, then management certifies. That is final.

The company partner share is never cut by investor side-deals. Those only move money inside the pool.

B · Special arrangements

A side deal — without changing ownership

Ownership stays 30 / 40 / 30. After the pool is split, a standing rule can move part of some people’s base share to someone else (often the founding owner).

Typical deal

“The owner receives 20% of every other shareholder’s monthly share.”

Or only named people. Or profit months only, not losses.

Same 50,000 pool, 20% of others → Owner

A pays 4,000 · B pays 3,000 · Owner receives +7,000.

Final: Owner 22,000 · A 16,000 · B 12,000. Pool still 50,000. Company still 50,000.

E · How to · Finance

Enter this month’s profit

  1. Sign in as Finance (email, password, then the code if asked).
  2. If ownership is not 100%, stop — ask Admin to fix Shareholders first.
  3. Open Monthly Periods → Enter Monthly Result.
  4. Choose year and month (cannot duplicate a month).
  5. Type Approved Monthly Net Profit from Odoo. Minus for a loss. Optional journal ref.
  6. Preview the pool and each person. Then Save & Calculate.
  7. Open the month. Check base, arrangement, final. Add a manual adjustment only for a true one-off, with a reason.
  8. Submit for Review. You cannot change figures until management sends it back.

E · How to · Management

Approve the month and notify investors

  1. Sign in as Super Admin or System Administrator.
  2. Open Approvals (or the bell). Open the submitted month.
  3. Check: Net Profit matches Odoo, split is the agreed %, finals add to the pool.
  4. Wrong → Return to draft with a reason. Finance fixes and re-submits.
  5. Right → Approve & Certify. Certificates issue. Reports go if email/WhatsApp is on (or Send Reports Now).
  6. When cash has actually been paid: Mark payment completed (record only — no bank).
  7. To correct a locked month before payment: Reopen with a reason; it becomes draft and must be certified again.

Operations · 1 · Monthly Periods

Use and configure the profit engine

Who: Finance enters and submits · Super Admin / Admin approve, reopen, mark paid, send reports · Menu: Operations → Monthly Periods

How to use — every month

  1. Confirm Shareholders Active ownership = 100%. If not, Save & Calculate stays disabled.
  2. Open Monthly Periods → Monthly Mudarabah Profit Distribution.
  3. Set Year and Month (one record per calendar month).
  4. Type Approved Monthly Net Profit (from Odoo). Use a minus for a loss. Optional Odoo reference and notes.
  5. Click Preview Distribution to see the pool and each person, then Save & Calculate (status: Draft).
  6. On the period page: check Original / Adjustment / Final. Add a one-off with Add Manual Adjustment (shareholder, amount, reason).
  7. If figures are wrong while still Draft: Edit figures or Recalculate.
  8. Click Submit for Finance / Management Review. Figures freeze until management returns them.
  9. Management: Approve & Lock (certificates issue) or Return to Draft (reason, min 5 characters).
  10. When cash has really been paid: Mark Payment Completed. If email failed: Send Reports Now / Resend Reports.
  11. To fix a locked month before payment: Reopen for Correction (reason min 10 characters). It becomes Draft and must be certified again.

How to configure

  1. System Settings → Profit & Capital: set Shareholders' Mudarabah share (%) (often 50). That splits Net Profit into the shareholders’ pool vs the managing-partner share.
  2. Same room: share value, total company shares, Murabaha — shown on the period, not used to split profit.
  3. Special Arrangements: standing bonuses apply on the next calculate (Draft only). Approved months do not change.
  4. System Settings → Email & SMTP: tick auto-send certificates when a period is approved; set report delivery day; fill SMTP.
  5. System Settings → WhatsApp: Twilio on if notices should go to the phone.
  6. System Settings → Certificates / Brand: wording, logo, signature used when you Approve & Lock.

Watch: duplicate month is blocked. Open capital withdrawals show a warning but do not block calculate. Prior month not approved is a warning, not a lock.

Operations · 3 · Shareholders

Use and configure the capital register

Who: Finance views and exports · Super Admin / Admin add, edit, import, portal · Super Admin only: Clean all shareholders · Menu: Operations → Shareholders

How to use

  1. Open Shareholders. The Active ownership tile must read 100% before any month can calculate.
  2. Register Shareholder: Name, Email, Country, Ownership %, Effective from. Optional phone, shares, capital, investment date.
  3. Tick Company owner if this person should be Super Admin once they have a login. Tick Active.
  4. Optional: Also create portal login + password, or later Save Portal Access.
  5. Save Shareholder. If the total is not 100%, you get a warning — rebalance others.
  6. To change %: edit with a new Effective from (history is kept). To exit: Deactivate (company owner cannot be deactivated).
  7. Export Excel for a backup. Filters: search, country, status, Has portal / No portal.
  8. First go-live load: Replace from Excel / CSV → tick Preview only first → then Replace Register from File (missing rows are deactivated).

How to configure

  1. System Settings → Profit & Capital: share value, total company shares, company-owned Murabaha assets. These drive the shares/capital columns and Auto-fill shares from ownership %.
  2. On import, you can override Company-owned assets (Murabaha) and set Ownership effective from.
  3. Download CSV template from the import page if the office sheet is empty.
  4. Super Admin only, last resort: import page Delete all shareholders & assets (type RESET SHAREHOLDERS). Wipes register, months, certificates, portal users; keeps staff Super Admins.

Watch: unique emails. Shares/investment must match derived values when total company shares is set. Last Super Admin cannot lose Company owner.

Operations · 6 · Special Arrangements

Use and configure a standing bonus

Who: Super Admin / Admin only (Finance does not see this menu) · effect shows on the period and the investor statement

How to use

  1. Open Special Arrangements.
  2. On Add Arrangement: give it a name (e.g. “Owner 20% from others”).
  3. Pick the Recipient (must be an active shareholder).
  4. Set Bonus % — percent of each source’s base share of the pool, not of full Net Profit.
  5. Set Effective from. Effective to optional (must be on or after from-date).
  6. Either tick Apply to all other shareholders or tick named Source shareholders (recipient cannot be a source).
  7. Tick Apply on profit and/or Apply on loss. Add notes if needed.
  8. Click Save Arrangement. Open a Draft month and Recalculate (or create a new period) to see it. Check before you approve.
  9. To stop: Deactivate on the row. To change: Edit.

How to configure

  1. There is no extra settings room — the form on this page is the configuration.
  2. You need active shareholders first (recipient and sources).
  3. Inactive recipient is skipped with a warning on the period form.
  4. Does not change ownership. Does not cut the managing-partner share.
  5. Approved / locked months stay as certified. Change the rule, then recalculate Draft only.

Example: pool 50,000 · 20% of others → Owner. Final: Owner 22,000 · A 16,000 · B 12,000. Pool still 50,000.

  2. Set title, intro, profit/loss labels, currency, number prefix, roster, signature name/title/image, legal footer. Use Preview before go-live.
  3. System Settings → Brand: logo and colours on the PDF.
  4. System Settings → Email & SMTP: auto-send when a period is approved.
  5. System Settings → WhatsApp: optional notice with or without PDF attach.

Unapproved months cannot be selected as the register month. Reopening a locked month sets send-status back to pending.

Operations · 8 · Mudarabah Summary

Use and configure the agreement table

Who: Finance and Admin · read-only rollup · Menu: Operations → Mudarabah Summary

How to use

  1. Open Mudarabah Summary to see recent calculated months (last 36), including Draft and In review — status is on each row.
  2. Columns: Period, Status, Net Profit (Odoo), Shareholders' pool (with %), managing-partner share, Distributed to SH.
  3. Click Open to jump to that period’s detail (statements, approve, exports).
  4. If the alert shows outstanding capital withdrawals, click Review withdrawals.
  5. Do not enter profit here. Use Monthly Periods for that. Use Reports History if you only want approved months.

How to configure

  1. System Settings → Profit & Capital: Shareholders' Mudarabah share (%) — the pool column uses this %.
  2. Company / partner name on the table comes from Brand / Mudarabah settings.
  3. Nothing else is configured on this screen — it reports what already calculated.

Distributed to SH is the sum of final amounts after arrangements. It should still add to the shareholders’ pool.

Operations · 9 · Reports History

Use and configure the approved archive

Who: staff · ordinary investors are redirected to My Reports · Menu: Operations → Reports History

How to use

  1. Open Reports History for approved months only (drafts are not listed).
  2. Scan Net Profit, pool, partner share, Payment Completed/Pending, approved date, Reports Sent.
  3. Click Distribution to open the period and download per-person Statement / PDF / Certificate.
  4. Shortcuts on the page: Mudarabah Summary, Certificates, Audit Report.
  5. If Reports Sent is blank, open the period → Send Reports Now.

How to configure

  1. System Settings → Email & SMTP: auto-send on approval; report delivery day of month; SMTP so Reports Sent actually fills.
  2. Payment column is set on the period with Mark Payment Completed (management) — it is a record, not a bank payment.
  3. Certificate look comes from Certificates + Brand settings (same as the Certificates menu).

This is the archive. In-progress months live under Monthly Periods and Mudarabah Summary.

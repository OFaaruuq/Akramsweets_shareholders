Akram Sweets · Analytics

01 · Purpose

A year dashboard — not where profit is calculated

Monthly Periods is where Finance enters Net Profit and the system splits it. Analytics is where the office (and each investor) reads the certified year.

Management

See profit vs what was paid to investors, who earned what, best and worst month.

Finance

Confirm approved months exist for the year and export CSV for the board pack.

Investor

Charts & Trends: my payout history, ownership, download my PDF — never anyone else’s money.

03 · Data rules

What the numbers actually are

RuleMeaning for the client
Charts + year table = approved onlyDraft and in-review months do not appear on the graphs.
Year dropdownFilters those approved months. Default = current year if it has data, else latest year with periods.
YTD Company ProfitSum of approved Net Profit (full company), not the shareholders’ pool.
Total DistributedSum of investors’ final amounts (after arrangements).
Display KPIsTyped revenues/expenses on tiles. Never used to split the month.
Workflow countsDraft / review / approved are all years, not the selected year.
Investor privacyNo stacked breakdown of others, no Display KPIs, no workflow inbox.

05 · Staff tour

What to click in a management meeting

  1. Open Analytics. Pick the year under review.
  2. Read the strip: how many profit vs loss months; total distributed.
  3. Combo chart: company P/L line vs distributed line.
  4. Stacked bar: who received what each month (arrangements already in the finals).
  5. Donut: latest approved split.
  6. Table: open a month if a number looks wrong. Export CSV for the pack.
  7. Owner: Update Dashboard Figures only if you want display revenues on the same page.

06 · Investor tour

Charts & Trends — my numbers only

Title on screen: My Share Analytics. Draft months never appear.

They can

  • Pick year; see latest payout, YTD, ownership, lifetime
  • Read payout vs trend charts
  • Open a month and download PDF
  • Export CSV of their own rows

They cannot

  • See other investors on the stacked chart (it is hidden)
  • See Display KPIs or period workflow
  • Change ownership or calculate a month

08 · How to use Analytics

Office steps

Finance / Management

  • Sign in → left menu Analytics
  • Choose the year
  • Read summary strip and combo chart
  • Use stacked bar for per-person month
  • Export CSV if needed
  • Click a period name to open Monthly Periods

Investor

  • Sign in → Charts & Trends
  • Choose year
  • Read my payout tiles and charts
  • Open a row → statement or PDF
  • Optional: Export CSV of my rows only

If the table is empty, no month is approved that year yet. Calculating a draft is not enough.
